01

Credit Risk & Loan Approval Analysis Dashboard

Excel · Power Query · Power BI · 250 applicants

I built a FOIR (Fixed Obligation to Income Ratio) scoring model from applicant-level data — income, EMI, employment type, and payment history — and classified every applicant into Low, Medium, or High risk, the way a credit analyst would before recommending approval.

The interesting finding: Self-Employed applicants carried the highest average FOIR (54.6%), but Salaried applicants accounted for more defaults. FOIR alone doesn't predict default — income stability matters just as much.

Indian Stock Market Trend & Volatility Analysis

Power Query · Power BI · 2007–2026 · SENSEX & NIFTY 50

Starting from a 340-column raw dataset covering ten Indian indices, I reduced it to the fields that mattered, corrected data-type and aggregation errors, and built a dashboard tracking price trend, RSI momentum, and daily volatility for SENSEX and NIFTY 50.

Both indices show a sharp, well-defined dip around 2020 — the COVID-19 crash — followed by a fast recovery, inside a 19-year upward trend.

UK Road Accident Analysis Dashboard

Power BI · 144,419 recorded accidents · YoY 2021–2022

A year-over-year comparison of national accident data, broken down by vehicle type, weather condition, road surface, and road type, with serious casualties plotted geographically across the UK.

80.71% of serious casualties happened in fine weather, on dry roads — adverse weather isn't the main driver here; exposure during normal conditions is. That's the kind of finding a risk report needs to state plainly, not smooth over.
